5. How to mark your own work

Cách tự chấm bài
  1. Finish the whole answer first. Then pick up a different colour pen. Làm xong cả bài rồi mới chấm, dùng bút khác màu.
  2. Find the mark scheme at the back of your study companion (page numbers are in your plan above). Tìm bảng chấm điểm ở cuối sách.
  3. Tick each row only if the idea is clearly in your answer. Different words are fine if the meaning is the same. Do not go over the maximum. Chỉ đánh dấu khi ý đó thật sự có trong bài.
  4. Compare with the model answer. Rewrite the step you missed, and bring that question to the session. So sánh với bài mẫu, viết lại bước còn thiếu.

6. Mock test day checklist

Chuẩn bị cho ngày kiểm tra
  • Two pens, a pencil, a ruler and an eraser for diagrams.
  • Read the command word first: State, Define, Explain, Analyse.
  • Diagrams: label both axes, label every curve, and show the old and new price and quantity.
  • Watch the clock: 30 minutes for Section A, about 20 minutes for each Section B question.
  • Never leave an MCQ blank. If you are not sure, rule out the wrong answers and choose.
  • Read your answers again in the last 5 minutes.
