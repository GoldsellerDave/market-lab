For teachers

Three levels, in this order. Choose the same good (trà sữa, bánh mì or chocolate) in both. Open the lesson on the computer connected to the projector.

LESSON 1 · LEVEL 0

The market opens

Buyer and business cards, how much to buy and make at each price, the class demand and supply curves, Robo-Trader deals and a Paper 1 style check.

Syllabus 2.1–2.4 and 3.6 · about 60–70 minutes

Open Level 0
LESSON 2 · LEVEL 1

The trading floor

A live market with one card each. Three rounds, the sealed envelope, a marked Paper 2 style debrief with AI marking, and a Paper 1 style check. Lesson grades out of 25.

Syllabus 2.1.1, 2.2.1, 2.3.1, 2.4.1–2.4.3 · about 60–70 minutes

Open Level 1
LESSONS 3–4 · LEVEL 2

The market with prizes

Everyone trades chocolate online for real prizes. Buyers have value cards; five shops have a supply schedule. Four rounds: find the price, menu prices (shortages and surpluses), a cacao harvest shock and Halloween. Choose candy or both products, and an advanced plan with offers and counter-offers, when you open the room.

Syllabus 2.1–2.4, bridging into 2.5 · two lessons of about 60 minutes · robots fill empty places
